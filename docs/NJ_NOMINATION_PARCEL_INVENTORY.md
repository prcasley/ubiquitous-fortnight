# NJ nomination parcel inventory

The **NJ nominated-area properties** navigation item opens an on-demand inventory for the 130 tract references in the connected NJ nomination snapshot. This is a parcel research workspace, not an announcement-provided owner/contact list, a sale listing service or a federal certification lookup.

- Select a tract (initially Perth Amboy), or choose **Build all 130 tracts**. Tracts already complete are skipped on an all-tract retry. Expired/unverified nomination evidence cannot start a new inventory.
- Retrieve a verified exact tract polygon, then POST the polygon to the statewide NJGIN parcel service. Count, ordered source identities and final count must reconcile. All parcel uses/sizes are retrieved; use/acreage/text controls filter the display afterwards.
- Tract-edge intersections are included. One PAMS_PIN is counted once across tracts, with the intersected tract IDs retained. Geometry pieces are combined without adding reported acreage. Conflicting source attributes are flagged.
- Coverage lists all 130 tracts, including unchecked, unavailable and partial results. Source errors, rejected geometry/IDs, changed counts and retrieval limits do not become complete coverage. Failed retries retain older records with an incomplete status and their original successful retrieval date.
- Browser bounds: 5,000 source rows per tract and 20,000 retained source rows per session. Source-record counts and unique parcel counts differ when parcels cross tracts. Export and reload for individual unchecked tracts if a session hits its limit; do not describe a bounded run as a statewide completed inventory.
- Owner fields, mailing addresses and contact fields are excluded from bulk parcel requests and stripped from retained parcel properties if a server returns extra attributes. **Look up published owner** and **Look up first 25 matching owners** use the existing exact-ID owner adapter on request. Names are public assessment observations, not verified title/beneficial ownership. A blank NJGIN record does not imply county records lack a published name.
- County-record, state property-research, source and Google Maps links accompany each parcel. These are research routes, not a matched deed or owner contact. County, municipality, block, lot and published deed book/page help with manual lookup.
- Organization-style assessment names connect to the existing local company dossier. Business contact status counts cited contacts saved and marked reviewed by the user. No phone/email is scraped, inferred or independently verified by this feature. Individual names are not indexed into company dossiers.
- Opening a parcel uses inspection/Deal Nav and preserves existing scan criteria and results. Inventory data and requested names stay in session memory; existing organization dossiers/contacts use localStorage. Nothing is sent to the hosted assistant or committed as a public owner/contact dataset.
- **Export local research & coverage** downloads JSON with all accepted inventory parcels, coverage/missing data, source facts/geometry, requested ownership observations and contact-review counts. It does not export dossier contact values. Keep the export local; the Pages welcome screen is not authentication.

Verification: unit fixtures cover reconciliation, POST geometry, owner-field exclusion, changing counts, repeated identities, outages, truncation/caps, geometry validation and cross-tract deduplication. Browser fixtures exercise all 130 references, display filters, exact owner lookup, cited contact dossiers, map/Deal Nav navigation without resetting the scan, export, missing coverage and 360/390px widths. These are synthetic tests, not a claim that live parcels or owner coverage were retrieved from the network-restricted workspace. The new inventory suite is registered in the normal publication/QA runner.

Local validation: 265 Node tests, 9 Python checks, all 14 registered browser suites, and the static build passed. After final inventory-only refinements, its browser suite passed 20 coverage/owner/contact/navigation checks, including the 130-reference run and stopping after three consecutive unavailable tracts. GitHub-hosted results and live parcel coverage are separate from these local synthetic results.
