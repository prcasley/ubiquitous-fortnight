# Easton and physical-screening reliability — October 29, 2026

This update addresses the executive review of build `27c7187`. It supersedes the building-source and address-only behavior in the October 28 Deal Nav notes.

## Changes

- **Northampton acreage and construction year:** discover `ASMT_ACREAGE` and `COM_YEAR_BUILT` from live layer metadata. Apply the requested acreage predicate on the server before count/retrieval. Recognize `LUC` / `LAND_USE_CODE` only when the layer publishes a coded-value domain; never invent meanings for undocumented numeric classifications. Descriptive industrial filters remain supported. Deal Nav defaults visibly to all assessment types and at least five acres; users can change either control.
- **County pagination:** request an object-ID snapshot under the same spatial/acreage/type predicate, reconcile it with the count, sort IDs numerically, and retrieve explicit batches with `orderByFields=<verified OID> ASC`. Reject duplicate, missing or unexpected identities. Keep safety caps and partial-result notices.
- **Cross-border neighborhoods:** check connected Lehigh/Northampton coverage and NJGIN when the five-mile envelope intersects those coverage bounds. Merge distinct provider/state/county/parcel keys, exclude the selected parcel, and include only map-reference distances of at most five miles. Show per-source completion and preserve successful sources when another fails. Restore the primary state/county controls afterward. These adapters do not provide complete coverage of all PA counties or other neighboring states.
- **Address identity:** query the county polygon layer using the geocoded point, then verify point containment locally. Accept only one containing published polygon. Show the requested address alongside the published parcel address and explain that point containment does not establish a building/address, title or legal-lot match. No match, multiple matches, truncated responses or unavailable sources leave identity unverified.
- **Public Northampton facts:** project published land/building values, recorded sale/date/validity code and `FLAG_10_KOZ`. Exclude sales of $1 or less and dates missing, invalid or equal to January 1, 1979. Sale-validity codes remain uninterpreted unless officially documented. Land + building assessment is an assessment observation, not a current market or asking price. PA KOZ is labeled separately from federal OZ. On-demand selected-parcel ownership lookup reads `OWNERS_NAME_1` and `MAIL_ADDRESS` directly from the county; it does not persist those values or put them into summaries, bulk scans or exports. No owner identity/contact inference or out-of-state residency inference.
- **Building measurements:** replace `USA_Structures_View` with OpenStreetMap building ways/multipolygon relations through a bounded Overpass query. Clip and union mapped polygons, including courtyard holes. Reject runtime-error/partial, excessive or malformed responses. Missing OSM coverage stays unknown, never zero buildings or vacancy. OSM can omit buildings, so measured coverage can understate roof coverage and cannot establish usable/paved storage yard. Display OSM attribution and carry source/license into shortlist exports. Converter is pinned, locally served, and accepts JSON responses; no remote converter scripts or local models.
- **Progress:** four parcel workers run the existing independent public checks in parallel. Overpass requests are limited to two in flight. Show a labeled progress bar, completed count, elapsed seconds and Stop. No guarantee of upstream service latency or completeness.
