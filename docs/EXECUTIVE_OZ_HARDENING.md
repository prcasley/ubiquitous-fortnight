## Parcel coverage is separate from tract nomination

NJ checks calculate geodesic overlap area against the union of intersecting nominated tract polygons. Overlapping tract pieces are not double-counted. A positive parcel screening match requires at least **95% of the mapped parcel area** inside the nominated-tract union. This is a conservative application screening rule, **not a legal threshold for OZ tax eligibility**. Tract nomination is not federal certification or project qualification.

Line/corner contact has zero area. Positive but sub-95% coverage is labelled partial/boundary review and excluded from the positive count. Point-only references cannot establish parcel coverage. Cards, shortlists, selected-property research and field packs share these labels and measured percentages. Old saved nomination observations without verified parcel-area coverage cannot retain a positive parcel label.

NJ evidence freshness is checked before and after retrieval, including searches with no intersections. Expired evidence displays “NJ nomination data expired; refresh needed,” and supports neither nomination nor absence claims. The selected-property OZ workspace uses the same check. Weekly evidence refresh and source/expiry guards remain in place.

## Broward acreage and reliability

Broward prefers verified published acreage/square-foot fields when metadata exposes one. Missing/nonpositive published sizes stay candidates for polygon measurement. Otherwise, a shape-area field is used for a conservative minimum-area prefilter only when metadata verifies Web Mercator meter units. Projected area is **not** presented as land acreage: final comparisons use the measured geodesic GIS polygon. If no supported prefilter can be justified, the countywide request is refused with town alternatives. Upper-bound-only filters need published size units or a town search. Existing caps and partial-result labels remain; raw candidate counts are not guaranteed final matches.
